# Review guide

Follow the [Definition of Done](../docs/contributing.md#6-tests-handoff-and-definition-of-done).

Review acceptance and the full diff: scope, permissions/membership, contract consistency, input/errors, secrets/logs, dependencies, generated files, and English documentation outside report/.

Separate automated passes, manual evidence, blocked prerequisites, and future work. Do not approve product capabilities based on infrastructure checks. No self-approval/merge without a reviewer. Do not enable CI/CD or deploy incidentally.
