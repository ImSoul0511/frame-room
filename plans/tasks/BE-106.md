## Scope and dependencies

Implement and verify the P1 collaboration, proofing, delivery, review, and timeline
endpoints on top of BE-105 and DB-104:

- Shoot room: `POST /v1/shoot-rooms/:id/messages`, `POST /v1/shoot-rooms/:id/milestones`,
  `PATCH /v1/shoot-rooms/:id/milestones/:milestoneId`, `POST /v1/shoot-rooms/:id/decisions`,
  `PATCH /v1/shoot-rooms/:id/decisions/:decisionId`.
- Proofing & gallery: `POST /v1/bookings/:id/gallery-images`,
  `POST /v1/bookings/:id/selections`, `POST /v1/bookings/:id/selections/reopen`.
- Deliveries & reviews: `POST /v1/bookings/:id/deliveries`,
  `POST /v1/bookings/:id/reviews`.
- Timeline: `GET /v1/me/timeline`.

No new SQL migration or Cloud deployment is included in this slice.
